# P02-USA-200 research gate

## Known

- Ballot comment: `P02-USA-200`
- Document: `DM32.2-2013-00032R2`
- Comment: 22
- Classification: Major Technical
- The comment identified that `<data change delta table>` lacked INCLUDE columns.
- The purpose described was passing extra information out of the data change statement, including information that could distinguish MERGE outcomes.
- No solution text has been found in the material currently available.

## Public ballot text

The publicly mirrored ballot compilation says that the standard's data-change-delta-table capability lacks the VLDB paper's INCLUDE-column functionality. It identifies the use case as passing additional information—such as the branch taken by a MERGE—from the data change statement to the containing SELECT, and recommends enhancing `<data change delta table>` accordingly.

The record gives **Solution: None provided with comment**. It therefore establishes the requirement and points to prior art, but does not establish adopted wording or a committee-approved design.

Public source: [DM32.2 CD 9075-* Ballot Comments, 2013-03-13](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=a47d9a12e846fbcbccf481afb81b40d6ebc79471&repid=rep1&type=pdf). This is a public mirror of the ballot compilation, not a current INCITS disposition record.

## Disposition

The ballot comment was converted at the September 2014 SC32 WG3 meeting in
Toronto into Language Opportunity FND-A20. The language opportunity was later
carried into the 2016 DIS ballot comments as `P02-USA-040` (MEM-017, sequence
8).

According to the June 2026 WG3 disposition recorded in MEM-054r1, sequence 8,
the language opportunity was deleted and the comment was closed with no action:
the proposed extension had not been taken forward by a working paper or other
technical action.

The available disposition correspondence is the current evidence for this
history. Obtain and cite the official MEM-054r1 record in a committee-facing
submission if it is available; do not present the correspondence itself as an
ISO/IEC or INCITS normative record.

## What the disposition means for this proposal

The no-action disposition does not adopt or reject particular syntax. The
proposal should cite P02-USA-200 and FND-A20 as prior recognition of the gap,
while presenting the current text as a new technical proposal with its own
syntax and semantics. It should not imply that the committee assigned this
repository the work or adopted an INCLUDE design.

The disposition does not adopt or reject particular syntax. It closes the
language opportunity without action, so this repository should present the
current proposal as a new technical proposal that addresses the previously
recognized gap, not as implementation of an adopted disposition.
